# Known gaps and risks

The largest untested risks are real Ukrainian speech and mixed-language clips; the rest are known limits. Add a gap when you find one; remove it, with a decision line, when it is fixed.

- **Mixed languages in one clip:** in one synthetic test the English half of a Ukrainian-then-English clip was dropped.
- **One place per report:** "the corner of A and B", "between A and B" and "at A, going to B" keep only one place. A crossing is found from one street named and the street the unit is on [117]; with no street known, "the corner of A and B" asks among the crossings of A. In "the pharmacy on rue de Fontenay" the street does not narrow the choice.
- **"The next crossing" outside a mission:** the phone does not send the street it is on or the way it is going, so with no mission the coordinator asks which crossing every time.
- **No shops, cafés, restaurants, banks or house numbers** in the gazetteer ([gazetteer.md](gazetteer.md#how-it-is-built)). Stops, fuel stations, pharmacies, hotels, water and named buildings are in.
- **A name shared by a hotel and a landmark goes to the nearest one.** From Maidan, "Я біля Дніпра" picks Hotel Dnipro, 250 m away, not the river; before hotels were in, it picked the Dnipro metro station, 2.6 km away. Saying "the river" picks the river. The model's guess of a category is not used unless the word was said [15].
- **A bridge with no name is not a place.** None of Izium's 12 road bridges has a name in OpenStreetMap, so «Я біля мосту» is not placed; the same holds for the other towns counted near the front. Other limits the Izium build showed are in [gazetteer.md](gazetteer.md#izium-added-saturday-21-november): «біля вокзалу» is read as the wrong kind of place, and English names barely exist there.
- **Tracks, not built yet:** a question when a new sighting is close to an old track (each unnumbered sighting opens a new track), "Track 3 is gone", range rings for a system, and any edit by the operator on the receiver [145].
- **Tracks, limits of what is built:** a negation is not understood, so "not hostile" marks a track hostile [148]. A count the model misses is not read from the words ("two suspicious vehicles" came back once with no count). Only the newest question is shown on a phone, so two track questions in a row hide the first. "Heading towards the bridge" gives no point and the app asks, even for a track that already has a place [149].
- **The French and Ukrainian words for tracks are unchecked:** "Piste 3" and "Об'єкт 3", the kinds and the adjectives were written without a native speaker. The Ukrainian voice cannot say digits, so the system voice reads the number [44]. The receiver's own words for tracks are English only.
- **Tracks are checked with typed reports and synthetic voices only:** in a desktop browser at 1440 × 900, through hosted Mistral and the local 3B model, and the tracks script's 11 clips through the coordinator. On 21 Nov the phone screens for a track were opened in the iPhone simulator, with ALPHA 1's clips sent from the transmitter, twice ([tracks.md](tracks.md#on-the-phone)). Not with a real phone, a person's voice, or a projector.
- **A track's symbol drawn off its point** ([tracks.md](tracks.md#when-symbols-would-overlap)) [167]: where a symbol would hide a unit or another track, it is drawn nearby with a line back. In a crowded spot it can end up well away from its point, and the line can cross another label. Label sizes are estimated from the letters, not measured.
- **Other voices in a clip:** the app cannot tell whose voice is whose; when two voices name different places it asks [154]. It finds voices only where Whisper on the phone marks them; Mistral's speech-to-text, as called today, marks none ([voices.md](voices.md)).
- **Whispered reports are untested:** whether Whisper and Voice Isolation keep a whisper, and whether loudness and voicing tell the speaker from other voices on real recordings. Test clips: [voices.md](voices.md#test-clips-155) [155, 156].
- **Spoken coordinates and grid references** are not parsed.
- **The local model can pin a place nobody named** ([evals/README.md](../evals/README.md#open), 21 Nov): Ministral 3 3B writes "Bérault", a name from the prompt's examples, for "Two blocks past the church" and "Je suis derrière l'hôtel de ville". Two places in one report, which pinned the place being left, is fixed on hosted Mistral; the local model still misreads "On rue de Fontenay, going to the pharmacy".
- **Other extraction misses, through hosted Mistral** ([evals/README.md](../evals/README.md), 21 Nov): "outside" is read as near; "Copy that, moving out" becomes a movement. None of these pins a wrong place. The local 3B model fails 15 of 65 cases every time, also with no wrong pin.
- **Real Ukrainian speech is untested;** all Ukrainian tests used a synthetic voice. Short clips sometimes came back misspelled, though the place still resolved.
- **Language tagging, for clients that do not send `language`:** the web page and the transmitter always send it [106]. Without it, an English sentence with a French street name was tagged as French, so a follow-up question would have been in French. A report in Latin letters is never tagged Ukrainian unless speech-to-text said so [104].
- **A pinned language that is wrong is not caught:** a phone left on Українська while someone speaks English makes Whisper write the English in Cyrillic, and the place is not found [106].
- **No retry on Mistral rate limits;** a refused call fails the report. Most calls take under a second, but one took 8.3 s against a 10 s timeout.
- **Interface text is English only;** only the question and place names follow the speaker's language [39].
- **The Ukrainian voice downloads 77 MB** the first time with nothing on screen to show it.
- **No auth;** the rate limit is per server instance and is not a security control. Anyone who can reach the coordinator can open, end or delete a mission, or join one under any free call sign.
- **A phone that switches coordinator while in a mission** is told its mission has ended, and has to join one on the new coordinator.
- **The live site lags `main`** until someone deploys.
- **The native app's choice list does not show the town** of two same-name streets; the spoken question does ([gazetteer.md](gazetteer.md#same-name-streets)).
