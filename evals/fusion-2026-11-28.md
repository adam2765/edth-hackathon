# Fusion of track sightings: simulation report

28 November 2026. Branch `claude/fusion`, from `origin/main` at `ec0c8a8`.

This is a simulation report. Nothing here comes from a real sighting, a real voice, a model or the
coordinator. `lib/geo/fusion.ts` is not imported by the app, the resolver or the command post.

**Final run:** commit `f59baf7e26aab501ca24958c30baacde000059bd`, seed 20261128, 2,000 trials for the main table, 1,000 for the correction comparison, 500 for the threshold sensitivity. Duration 1,706 s (28 min 26 s) on this Windows computer. Command: `npx tsx scripts/sim-fusion.ts --trials 2000`. The report commit follows the code commit; the hash is the code the run used.

## What was measured

- **Scenarios:** perpendicular bearings, nearly parallel bearings, three bearings with one gross
  outlier, three observers with range and bearing, and one observer with five reports. A straight
  true path is drawn for each trial; observers are at fixed local places.
- **Trials:** 2,000 per row, seed 20261128. The same seed gives the same tables.
- **Truth and estimator are separate.** In the 2/5/10 rows the estimator is set to the truth's
  sigma (matched), not fixed at 5°. The mismatch rows fix the estimator at 5° while the truth is
  10°, has a constant +3° bias, understates range by 20%, or puts 10% of bearings at five times
  the sigma.
- **Noise:** observer position σ = 10 m per axis; range σr = 0.25·r; bearing σθ as the truth model
  says. These are hypotheses, not measurements.
- **Metrics:** CEP50 and CEP90 are the median and 90th percentile of radial error; NEES is the
  squared error normalised by the estimate, expected 2 in 2D. Each cell is point estimate and 95%
  interval: bootstrap for CEP and NEES, Wilson for circle coverage. The 50% and 90% circles are
  the smallest circles around the estimate that hold that share of its posterior.
- **Bearing-only geometry:** a coarse 50 m grid over the prior locates the ridge; a 10 m grid around
  its mean gives the metres. The range prior is explicit (`uniform-area` by default, `uniform-r` as
  the alternative), and the maximum range is explicit. A result that reaches the grid or prior edge,
  whose 90% circle exceeds a quarter of the maximum range, or whose observers' baseline is too short
  for the range (less than `tan(15°) × maxRange`) is `unresolved`, not a point.
- **Current method:** one report with the resolver's circle `max(50, 0.4·r)`. The *exact single*
  witness is the same report with its continuous bearing and no fusion. The *8-point* witness
  rounds that bearing to 45° as the resolver does.

## Robust corrections

The baseline and five corrections, on the same five mismatch cases, 1,000 trials each. Each cell
is `90% circle / NEES`; the full 50% and interval columns are in the simulator output. The
estimator is fixed at 5? in every row; the truth model changes.

| case | baseline 5? | sigma mix 2/5/10 | Student-t ?=3 | Student-t ?=4 | Student-t ?=5 | inflation ?2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| matched 5? | 91% / 1.84 | 100% / 1.13 | 99% / 0.73 | 99% / 0.93 | 98% / 1.08 | 99% / 0.92 |
| truth 10? | 47% / 7.82 | 100% / 4.72 | 83% / 3.10 | 72% / 3.96 | 66% / 4.63 | 75% / 3.84 |
| bias +3? | 83% / 2.74 | 100% / 1.69 | 98% / 1.07 | 96% / 1.38 | 95% / 1.61 | 97% / 1.36 |
| heavy tails | 80% / 5.72 | 100% / 2.87 | 92% / 2.04 | 91% / 2.65 | 89% / 3.11 | 91% / 2.83 |
| range 0.8? | 80% / 2.73 | 100% / 1.91 | 93% / 1.65 | 92% / 1.77 | 91% / 1.86 | 91% / 1.77 |

The sigma mixture (prior 0.25/0.5/0.25 over 2/5/10) is rejected: it covers 100% at both levels in
every case, and its NEES stays above 2 on truth 10? and heavy tails because the mean is still
pulled by the 5? component. It is conservative, not corrected.

Student-t ?=3 is closest to nominal overall: mean absolute deviation of the 90% circle from 90 is
5.8 points, against 14.2 for the baseline. It brings truth 10? from 47% to 83%, heavy tails from
80% to 92%, and the range error from 80% to 93%. Its cost is the matched case: 99% at 90 and NEES
0.73, so it is deliberately conservative there. Student-t ?=4 is the less conservative compromise
(matched NEES 0.93, truth 10? 72%); ?=5 and covariance inflation ?2 sit between the two. If the
priority is not to over-state certainty on a wrong noise model, ?=3 is the pick; if the priority is
to keep the matched case closer to nominal, ?=4. Neither is a default: the choice belongs to the
person wiring the ghost layer.

## Unresolved thresholds

500 trials per row, ? = 5?. The thresholds are the minimum bearing spread (10?, 15?, 25?) and the
share of the maximum range over which a 90% circle counts as too wide (0.25 or 0.5).

| scenario | 10? / 0.25 | 10? / 0.5 | 15? / 0.25 | 15? / 0.5 | 25? / 0.25 | 25? / 0.5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| perpendicular | 0% unresolved, CEP50 73.8 m | same | same | same | same | same |
| nearly parallel | 100% unresolved | same | same | same | same | same |

The two geometries sit far from the thresholds: the perpendicular bearings span 90? and the nearly
parallel baseline is 200 m against a 2,000 m prior. The sensitivity does not distinguish 10, 15 and
25? here, and the wide fraction does not either. Intermediate geometries, where the spread is near
15? or the circle near a quarter of the prior, are not simulated; the thresholds should be checked
there before they are trusted.

## Calibration and significance

At 95%, an interval that contains the nominal value is not a significant departure.

- **Perpendicular, 2° and 5°:** NEES contains 2, and both circle intervals contain 50% and 90%. No
  significant departure.
- **Perpendicular, 10°:** NEES contains 2, but the 50% circle is 54% [52-56] and the 90% circle is
  93% [92-95]. Both over-cover significantly. 13% of trials are unresolved.
- **Three bearings + outlier:** NEES is 2.17 at 2° (significantly over 2) and 1.69 at 10°
  (significantly under 2). The 50% circle over-covers at 5° (56%) and 10° (59%). The 90% circle is
  not significantly off at any sigma.
- **Three observers, range:** at 5°, NEES is 2.34 [2.22-2.45] (significantly over 2), the 50%
  circle is 46% [44-48] and the 90% circle is 86% [85-88], both significantly under. At 10°, NEES
  2.52 and the circles 47%/85% are also significantly off. At 2°, NEES 1.74 is significantly under
  2 and the 50% circle is 55%, significantly over.
- **One observer, five reports:** NEES is 0.35 to 0.40, all intervals well below 2; the 50% circle
  is 63% to 74% and the 90% circle 93% to 94%, both over-covering. This is the intended prudence of
  covariance intersection, not a calibration bug. The earlier 0.21 at 200 trials was the same
  property with a wider interval; at 2,000 it settles at 0.35 [0.32-0.38].
- **Mismatch:** the 10°/5° case (NEES 7.97, 90% circle 46%), the 3° bias (2.81, 82%), the heavy
  tails (7.21, 81%) and the range 0.8× case (2.56, 83%) are all far outside their nominal levels.
- **Nearly parallel:** no point estimate, so no coverage or NEES to report. That is the correct
  result: the range is not observed.

## Measured and not measured

**Measured (simulation only):**

- Five scenarios, 2,000 trials per row, truth 2/5/10° matched and four mismatch models.
- Perpendicular bearings calibrate at 2° and 5°; at 10° the circles over-cover.
- A wrong noise model is severely over-confident; the 3° bias and 20% range error are also
  significant.
- The range prior choice does not change the perpendicular result; neither prior resolves the
  nearly parallel geometry.
- The continuous-angle witness separates the resolver's 45° rounding from fusion: at 5°, 283.8 m
  (8-point), 128.5 m (exact single), 56.8 m (fused).
- One observer's five reports are conservative (NEES 0.35 to 0.40), never smaller than five
  independent reports; the unit test checks the covariance area over several sets.
- The three area centres give identical results.
- Sigma mixtures are too conservative; Student-t ?=3 is closest to nominal overall (mean 90% deviation 5.8 points against 14.2), while ?=4 is the less conservative compromise. Neither is made a default.
- The unresolved thresholds 10/15/25? and 0.25/0.5 of the prior do not change the perpendicular or nearly parallel results; intermediate geometries are not tested.

**Not measured:**

- Real bearings, ranges, observer circles or voices. The noise values, including the 5° human
  sigma, are assumptions.
- Association on a live mission log: the χ² gate, Hungarian assignment and ambiguity are
  unit-tested, not run on real tracks.
- A moving target between reports, more than one target at a time, or a track that is lost.
- Grid north or magnetic north; only geographic north is modelled.
- Any display in the command post. Nothing is wired.

## Logging (built)

The fusion adapter cannot rebuild an observation from today's log alone:

- `TrackState.from` keeps only `{ lat, lon }`, so the observer's own radius at the sighting is lost.
  `ReportEntry.lastPosition` has a radius, but it is the transmitter's last position, not
  necessarily the observer at that report.
- The continuous bearing is not stored. It exists in the transcript and is re-read by
  `clockSaid`/`bearingSaid`; a `compass8` report and an exact bearing are otherwise
  indistinguishable.

The fields are now written at save time, with no new entry kind and no change to placement:

```ts
// lib/reports/types.ts, TrackSighting
from: { lat: number; lon: number; radius_m: number } | null;
/** The continuous bearing said, or the centre of the compass bin. */
bearing_deg: number | null;
bearing_source: "degrees" | "mils" | "clock" | "compass8" | null;
```

- `from.radius_m` is `lastPosition.radius_m ?? lastPosition.uncertainty_m ?? 0` when the report is
  saved.
- `bearing_deg` is `clockSaid(transcript).deg` or `bearingSaid(transcript).deg`; for a compass word
  only, the centre of its 45° bin, with `bearing_source: "compass8"`.
- Old entries lack the fields and read as null. Replay, the map and the resolver ignore them. Tests in `tests/sightingLog.test.ts` read an old sighting unchanged and a new one with all three fields.

Changes:

- `lib/reports/types.ts`: the three fields on `TrackSighting`; `TrackState.from` gains optional
  `radius_m`, `bearing_deg` and `bearing_source` for the latest sighting. No other change to
  `ReportEntry`.
- `lib/reports/fold.ts`: `seeTrack` copies the new fields into `TrackState.from` with the point. The
  append-only report entries already hold every sighting, so the adapter can read them by
  `track.n`; no per-track observation list is added.
- `lib/reports/record.ts` or the pipeline: fill the fields when a track sighting is saved, from the
  observer's last position and the transcript. `lib/geo/resolver.ts` is not touched.
- The log and the journal: still one `report` entry per report; the new fields are optional. No
  migration, no version bump, no new file.

Until the app writes these fields, the adapter must treat `sigma_m` as 0 and `bearing_source` as
`"compass8"` for old entries, and say so in its output. That is a known loss, not a silent
assumption.
