# Fusion of track sightings: simulation report

28 November 2026. Branch `claude/fusion`, from `origin/main` at `ec0c8a8`.

This is a simulation report. Nothing in this table comes from a real sighting, a real voice, a
model or the coordinator. The module `lib/geo/fusion.ts` is not imported by the app, the resolver or
the command post: it is a ghost estimate, computed and compared only.

## What was measured

- **Truth:** five synthetic scenarios, each with a straight true path and a fixed set of observers.
  The path point is drawn for each trial; observers are at known local places.
- **Measurements:** observer position noise σ = 10 m per axis; range noise σr = 0.25·r; bearing
  noise σθ = the human value (2°, 5° or 10°) because the source is a stated bearing in degrees.
- **Trials:** 200 per row, seed 20261128. The same seed gives the same tables.
- **Fusion:** local east/north plane at the area's centre, 10 m grid, half-size 1,000 to 2,000 m by
  scenario, outlier mixture ε = 0.05, same-observer reports inside 120 s intersected, different
  observers multiplied.
- **Current method:** one report with the resolver's own circle, `max(50, 0.4·r)` for a stated
  distance and bearing. A bearing alone places nothing today: the current method asks "how far".
- **Metrics:** CEP50 and CEP90 are the median and 90th percentile of the radial error over the
  trials. NEES is the squared error normalised by the estimated covariance; in 2D the expected mean
  is 2. "50% circle" and "90% circle" are the share of trials whose truth falls inside the circle
  the posterior announces for that level; the expected shares are 50% and 90%. "Cut off" is the share
  of posteriors with more than 0.2% of their mass on the grid's outer edge.

The parameters are hypotheses, not measurements. They have not been calibrated on real bearings,
real ranges, real observer circles or real voices.

## Results

### Two bearings, about perpendicular

Area: Vincennes. Grid half-size 1,500 m. Current method: no placement (asks how far).

| σθ human | fused CEP50 | fused CEP90 | NEES | 50% circle | 90% circle | cut off |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2° | 30.0 m | 55.4 m | 2.05 | 49% | 89% | 0% |
| 5° | 75.8 m | 135.9 m | 1.96 | 48% | 93% | 0% |
| 10° | 157.2 m | 310.5 m | 2.29 | 46% | 92% | 2% |

At 5°, the covariance is close to calibrated (NEES 1.96) and the 90% circle holds 93% of the
truths. The 50% circle holds 48%, within the noise of 200 trials.

### Two bearings, nearly parallel

Area: Vincennes. Grid half-size 2,000 m. Current method: no placement (asks how far).

| σθ human | fused CEP50 | fused CEP90 | NEES | 50% circle | 90% circle | cut off |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2° | 129.7 m | 357.8 m | 1.84 | 74% | 94% | 82% |
| 5° | 304.5 m | 624.9 m | 2.99 | 55% | 89% | 80% |
| 10° | 482.5 m | 723.6 m | 3.71 | 30% | 93% | 83% |

This is the weak case. The ellipse is long (CEP90 358 to 724 m), so there is no false precision,
but 80 to 83% of posteriors reach the grid's edge: the range is not observed, and the finite window
cuts the ridge. The NEES above 2 at 5° and 10° says the cut posterior is over-confident, not that a
long ellipse is precise. Treat a cut-off result as unresolved, not as a measurement.

### Three bearings and one gross outlier

Area: Vincennes. Grid half-size 1,200 m. The fourth report is 100° off and 2.5 times too far.
Current method: no placement (asks how far).

| σθ human | fused CEP50 | fused CEP90 | NEES | 50% circle | 90% circle | cut off |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2° | 14.6 m | 34.3 m | 1.89 | 55% | 91% | 0% |
| 5° | 33.3 m | 75.0 m | 1.88 | 57% | 91% | 0% |
| 10° | 53.0 m | 155.9 m | 1.67 | 58% | 92% | 0% |

The mixture keeps the outlier out. The unit test measures the same report with ε = 0: the estimate
moves 48 m from the three good bearings; with ε = 0.05 it stays within 5 m.

### Three observers, bearing and distance

Area: Vincennes. Grid half-size 1,200 m. The current method uses the first report only, with the
resolver's `max(50, 0.4·r)` circle (no stated confidence level).

| σθ human | fused CEP50 | fused CEP90 | NEES | 50% circle | 90% circle | cut off | current CEP50 | current CEP90 | truth inside the current circle |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2° | 22.8 m | 41.5 m | 1.59 | 57% | 93% | 0% | 109.6 m | 278.4 m | 89% |
| 5° | 61.9 m | 109.7 m | 2.41 | 44% | 85% | 0% | 136.5 m | 327.6 m | 83% |
| 10° | 93.1 m | 179.4 m | 2.46 | 46% | 86% | 0% | 172.1 m | 333.3 m | 80% |

Fusion cuts CEP50 by about half against one report. At 5° the 90% circle holds 85%, slightly under
its nominal level; the NEES of 2.4 says the same in another way.

### One observer, five reports in two minutes

Area: Vincennes. Grid half-size 1,000 m. All five reports are inside the 120 s correlation window,
so they are intersected, not multiplied. The current method uses one report.

| σθ human | fused CEP50 | fused CEP90 | NEES | 50% circle | 90% circle | cut off | current CEP50 | current CEP90 | truth inside the current circle |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2° | 48.0 m | 111.0 m | 0.25 | 65% | 94% | 0% | 69.4 m | 156.5 m | 90% |
| 5° | 50.3 m | 113.7 m | 0.21 | 70% | 96% | 0% | 75.4 m | 155.4 m | 89% |
| 10° | 68.4 m | 139.0 m | 0.35 | 67% | 93% | 0% | 103.4 m | 184.5 m | 81% |

The covariance does not shrink like five independent reports: NEES is 0.21 to 0.35, well below 2,
so the circle is conservative and covers more than its nominal level. The median error still falls
because the ranges are averaged; the uncertainty does not pretend to fall with them.

### The same perpendicular geometry at the three area centres

σθ human 5°. The local plane is built from `bearingDeg`, `distanceM` and `destination`, so north is
geographic north and there is no UTM convergence term.

| area | fused CEP50 | fused CEP90 | NEES | 50% circle | 90% circle |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vincennes | 75.8 m | 135.9 m | 1.96 | 48% | 93% |
| Kyiv | 75.8 m | 135.9 m | 1.96 | 48% | 93% |
| Izium | 75.8 m | 135.9 m | 1.96 | 48% | 93% |

The identical rows are expected: the same local geometry was placed at each centre. They check that
the plane does not rotate north or accumulate a longitude-dependent error.

## Measured and not measured

**Measured (simulation only):**

- The five scenarios above, 200 trials per row, three values of σθ.
- Perpendicular bearings: calibrated at 5° (NEES 1.96, 90% circle 93%).
- Nearly parallel bearings: a long ellipse, but 80 to 83% of posteriors cut off; over-confident
  when cut.
- One gross outlier: no move of the estimate with ε = 0.05; 48 m of move with ε = 0.
- Five reports from one observer: covariance stays conservative (NEES 0.21 to 0.35), median error
  improves against one report.
- The three area centres give the same result.

**Not measured:**

- Real bearings, ranges, observer circles or voices. The noise values are assumptions.
- The human σθ of 5°; the sensitivity shows what changes between 2° and 10°, not which one is true.
- Association on a real mission log: the Hungarian gate and the ambiguity result are unit-tested,
  not run on live tracks.
- A moving target between reports, more than one target at a time, or a track that is lost.
- Grid north or magnetic north: only geographic north is modelled.
- The distance at which the local plane's curvature matters; it grows with distance and is
  negligible at the city scale, but it is not a projected grid.
- Any display in the command post. Nothing is wired.

## How to run it again

```bash
npx tsx scripts/sim-fusion.ts                 # 200 trials per row, seed 20261128
npx tsx scripts/sim-fusion.ts --trials 500
npm test -- tests/fusion.test.ts
```

The unit tests cover the perpendicular and nearly parallel cases, the outlier, the same-observer
correlation, the local plane in the three areas, the association gate and ambiguity, and the fixed
seed.
